# Exercise 1.1-5

Come up with a real-world problem in which only the best solution will do. Then come up with one in which a solution
that is "approximately" the best is good enough.

# Answer

**A real-world problem in which only the best solution will do:**

**Organ transplant matching**

When a donated organ becomes available, doctors must determine __the best possible recipient__ based on factors such as
blood type, tissue compatibility, urgency, and distance.
If the match is not optimal, the organ may be rejected or may not reach the patient in time. Because lives depend on
this decision, __only the best (optimal) solution is acceptable__.

**A real-world problem in which an approximately best solution is good enough:**

**Routing a delivery truck**

A shipping company wants to find a route for a truck that delivers packages to many locations.
Finding the exact shortest route is extremely expensive (Traveling Salesman Problem), but in practice, a route that is __close to optimal__ —maybe only 5–10% longer— is perfectly acceptable.
It still saves fuel, time, and money, and the small difference does not significantly impact operations.
