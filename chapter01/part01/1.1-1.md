# Exercise 1.1-1

Give a real-world example that requires sorting or a real-world example that requires computing a convex hull.

# Answer
For sorting: Sorting hospital patients by priority
We need to sort patients from the highest priority to the lowest priority. It will allow doctors to treat the most critical patients first.

For computing a convex hull: Drones mapping a forest boundary
Imagine many drones flying over a forest to collect GPS coordinates of the trees. But we don't know the shape of the forest, and we want to know outer boundary of the forest.
Then we need to compute the convex hull of all GPS points.
The convex hull gives the smallest polygon that encloses all the trees.
