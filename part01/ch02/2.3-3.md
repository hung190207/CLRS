# Exercise 2.3-3

Use mathematical induction to show that when *n* is an exact power of 2, the solution of the recurrence

$$
T(n) =
\begin{cases}
2, & \text{if } n = 2, \\
2T(n/2) + n, & \text{if } n = 2^k, \; k > 1.
\end{cases}
$$

is $T(n) = nlgn$.

# Answer

We prove that for every $n = 2^k$ with $k \ge 1$,

$$
T(n) = nlgn.
$$

### 1. Base Case
When $n = 2$:

$$
T(2) = 2
$$

Right-hand side:

$$
2 lg 2 = 2 * 1 = 2
$$

=> Base case matches.
